## 📊 What You Can Do With VeloceNet-Studio

VeloceNet-Studio packs powerful network tools into one easy screen. Here's what each feature does for you:

### ⏱️ Live Latency Matrix

This shows your ping (response time) to different servers in real time. A lower number means a snappier connection—great for online gaming or video calls. The colorful grid updates every second so you can spot problems instantly.

### 🧭 Traceroute

Ever wonder where your data travels? Traceroute maps the path your internet traffic takes from your computer to any website or server. If something is slow, this tool pinpoints exactly which hop along the route is causing the delay.

### 📶 Bandwidth Monitor

Watch your internet speed live. This meter shows upload and download speeds in real time, so you can see if your connection is actually delivering what your internet plan promises. Perfect for checking if background downloads are hogging your bandwidth.

### 🛰️ Warp Scanner

This special scanner checks for WireGuard VPN servers near you. If you use a VPN, this helps you find the fastest connection point automatically. It scans quickly and shows you the best options at a glance.
